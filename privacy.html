Privacy Policy

Minimalista: Home Design Decor (“Minimalista Home”, “the app”) is made by Lunar Sprint (“we”). This policy explains what the Android app does with your information.

Effective 11 November 2026 · Updated for the setup questions

In short

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads.
  • Your plans, furniture layouts, imported pictures and renders are stored only on your phone. We don't have a server that receives them.
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports are on by default, and you can turn each off in Settings › Privacy.
  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

1. Information stored on your phone

Everything you create stays in the app's private storage on your device: plans, rooms, walls, furniture, floor and paint choices, project names, plan pictures you import (photos or PDF pages), your answers to the five setup questions (for example “Redecorate a room” or “Japandi”) and app settings. Renders and images you save go to your phone's Pictures folder (“Minimalista Home”) or wherever you choose to share them.

The app turns off Android cloud backup, so this data isn't copied to Google Drive. If you use Android's phone-to-phone transfer when you set up a new phone, your plans can be copied directly to the new phone. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, it is deleted from your phone.

Setup answers are sent as fixed choices from a list, such as “move” or “japandi”, never as free text. These events never include your plans, project or room names, pictures, renders or anything you type. Firebase uses an app-instance identifier that is reset when you reinstall the app. It isn't linked to your name, email or Google account. Advertising ID access is removed from the app.

Firebase is provided by Google. See Firebase privacy and security. Analytics data is kept for 14 months. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

3. Camera and “see it in your room”

The camera is used only when you open “See in your room” and allow access. Camera frames are processed on your phone by Google Play Services for AR (ARCore) to find the floor. We don't record, store or upload camera images. Google's handling of ARCore data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Photos and files you import

When you import a floor plan, you pick a photo or PDF with Android's own picker. The app only gets the file you chose. Walls are detected on your phone and the picture is kept with that plan on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

5. AI photo redesign

The current version of the app does not send photos anywhere. Style previews (“Try a style”) are made on your phone. If a future version adds cloud AI photo redesign, it will ask before sending a photo and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

6. Purchases

Minimalista Home Pro subscriptions are sold and processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The app receives from Google Play only whether you have an active subscription, so it can unlock Pro on your phone. See the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Third-party content

3D furniture models come from Poly Haven (polyhaven.com) under the CC0 licence and are bundled in the app. Opening the app does not contact Poly Haven.

8. Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Your rights and deleting data

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your information. Because we don't hold an account or your content, most data can be removed directly on your phone. See how to delete your data, or email us and we will help with anything held by Firebase.

For anonymous usage statistics we rely on our legitimate interest in improving the app, and you can object at any time by turning the setting off.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
