Delete your data

Minimalista: Home Design Decor by Lunar Sprint has no account, so there is no account to delete. Your plans live on your phone and you can remove them yourself at any time.

Delete plans on your phone

One plan

  1. Open Minimalista Home.
  2. On the home screen (“Your homes”), scroll to Recent, then long-press the plan or tap its ⋮ menu.
  3. Tap Delete and confirm.

Everything

  1. Open Android Settings › Apps › Home Design.
  2. Tap Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

This deletes all plans, imported pictures, your setup answers and settings from the app immediately. Renders you saved to your Pictures folder are normal photos, so delete them in your gallery app.

Stop and delete analytics and crash data

  1. Turn off Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage and Send crash reports in the app. Nothing more is sent.
  2. To delete what was already sent, email lunarsprintapps@gmail.com with the subject “Minimalista Home data deletion”.

Because this data is anonymous, we will ask for the approximate date you used the app and your device model so we can find it. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. If we can't find matching data, we will tell you. Otherwise, analytics data expires automatically after 14 months and crash reports after 90 days.
