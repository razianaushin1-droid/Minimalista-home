How it starts

Five questions. Your first plan.

Tell the app what you're planning and it picks a matching layout, loads your style and opens the editor. You can change every answer later, or skip straight to drawing.

What you can do

Everything for planning a home, on your phone

Draw or import

Walls snap to 10 cm and 45°. Start from a template, or turn a photo or PDF of a floor plan into walls on your phone.

Furnish in real sizes

Sofas, beds, kitchens, bathrooms, lights and garden pieces, all in real centimetres, so you know what fits.

Try six styles

Japandi, Scandi, Industrial, Boho, Modern and Coastal recolour walls, floors and furniture in one tap. Undo puts it back.

Walk through in 3D

Walk round your plan in first person, render 4K stills in day, golden-hour or night light, and add floors, stairs and a roof.

Pick up where you left off

Your homes, one tap away

Your latest plan waits on the home screen. Open it to keep editing, or walk straight into it in 3D. On phones with ARCore you can also place a piece at real size in your room through the camera.

Your plans stay on your phone

No account and no cloud sync. Drawing, importing, 3D and rendering all run on your phone. Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports help us improve, and you can turn both off in Settings › Privacy. Read the privacy policy.

Unlimited drawings and plan imports, every template including the two-storey house, and the Pro furniture. Monthly or yearly through Google Play, at the price shown in the app.
